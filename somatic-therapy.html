What it is like

You do not have to arrive with it figured out.

01

Arriving

A conversation about where you are now and what you are carrying. No exhaustive history required in the first session.

02

Tracking

We follow where the old strategies live in your body. Posture, breath, the reflex to brace.

03

Working

Slow, relational, paced by you. Nothing gets pushed open before there is enough safety to hold it.

04

Integrating

What changes gets practiced in real decisions and real relationships, not only in the room.

Relational Therapy

Many people move between the two. Individual work builds the ground, relational work is where it gets tested.
