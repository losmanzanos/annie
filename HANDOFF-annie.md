## Writing and publishing journal posts

You can write and publish posts yourself, without me.

1. Go to **https://rootedresonancetherapy.com/admin**
2. Sign in with GitHub
3. Open **Journal Posts**, then either pick an existing post or create a new one
4. Write, then **Save**. The site rebuilds itself in a couple of minutes.

### A few notes on the editor

**Post** is one box per paragraph. Click to add another.
Start a box with `## ` to make it a heading.
Start a box with `> ` to make it a pull quote.

**Published** is a real switch. Turning it off doesn't just hide the post from the
list, it removes the page entirely, so a link someone saved will stop working.
Turning it back on brings it back.

**Web address** is the part after the slash. Changing it on a post that's already
live will break any link anyone has to it, so it's best left alone once published.

**Search engine description** is what shows up underneath your title in Google.
Around 155 characters is right.

## How people will find you

Your site is built to be found. Every page carries a proper title and description,
structured data that tells Google what each offering is and where you're licensed,
and a plain-language summary for AI search tools. Your sitemap has been submitted.

**This takes time.** A new address starts from nothing with Google, and it's
normally weeks rather than days before you see much. Redirecting your old domain
is what carries across the history you've already built.

### The two things that will help most

**Your Google Business Profile.** This is what puts you in local results when
someone searches for a therapist nearby. Set it up as a service area business, list
all three states you're licensed in, and add each offering as both a Service and a
Product — Products allow much more description and an image.

**Writing.** Every journal post is another page that can answer somebody's
question. You don't need to write often. Writing well about the things you actually
think about will do more than any technical work I could do.

## Important: therapy and wellness are kept separate

Every page distinguishes your licensed clinical work from your wellness offerings,
and this is deliberate.

**Therapy** — somatic and relational therapy. Psychotherapy under your LPC
licences, available only to residents of **Ohio, Arizona and Colorado**.

**Wellness** — sound healing, breathwork, workshops. Educational and wellness
services, available **anywhere**, and explicitly not psychotherapy, diagnosis or
crisis care.

Every wellness page says this in plain language, and the footer of every page
repeats it. If you add new offerings later, keep them on the right side of that
line, and say which side they're on.

Crisis resources (988 and others) appear on the contact page.

## Security, and one favour

Please change your Porkbun password. It was sent by email, and it's the same one
as your Gmail. If that mailbox is ever compromised, whoever gets in controls both
your email and your web address.

A password manager will generate and remember different ones for each account.

Also worth doing: turn on two-factor authentication for Porkbun, Cloudflare, GitHub
and Google, pointed at **your** phone.

One more thing — your old domain still carries a Google verification record from a
previous marketing company, which may mean they can still see your search data.
Chad will clear that when the transfer completes.
