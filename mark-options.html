Why these two

Both had to mean something specific, not just decorate.

Somatic: a plumb line coming to rest

A weight on a line, with the swing dying away to either side. It reads as settling, finding centre, weight dropping into the body.

It is also a quiet nod to pendulation, a real term of art in somatic work. Meaningful to her, invisible to everyone else.

Relational: two arcs meeting in the middle

Facing curves with a shared point of contact between them. Attunement rather than anatomy.

Deliberately not two little heads. Given that she works with non-monogamous clients, a mark that hard-codes a couple is the wrong signal.

One alternate, if you want to go further

Three overlapping rings instead of two facing arcs.

Relational, alternate: three rings

Overlapping fields rather than a pair. Says relationships of all kinds without putting a word to it.

Stronger signal to her actual client base. Slightly riskier: three rings is a common mark shape, so it leans a little more generic than the arcs.

My pick

The plumb line for somatic and the two facing arcs for relational. The arcs are more distinctive than the three rings, and both new marks carry the same ripple language as the sound healing mark you already liked, which is what finally makes the five read as one set rather than five choices.
