So many of us learned how to earn love before we ever learned how to know ourselves.

We adapted, performed, and shaped ourselves to fit the expectations of our families, partners, and culture, often losing sight of who we are along the way.

Rooted Resonance offers you a place to take the long walk back, and come home to yourself. No matter how far away you have gotten, I offer you a place to return to what has always been true.

Who I work with

I find myself working with people who have spent years adapting to keep relationships, avoid conflict, earn love, meet expectations, or all of the above. Most arrive feeling lost, worried about the future and unsure of their place in it. They are exhausted, burnt out from trying to fit in, and they don't know how to change it.

I am a queer, ethically non-monogamous, kink and sex positive Licensed Professional Counselor in the states of Ohio, Arizona, and Colorado. Over the past 6 years I have worked in private practice serving LGBTQIA+, neurodivergent, and kinky communities, assisting people in and out of relationships of all kinds to find who they are in the midst of feeling pressured to be like everyone else.

Scope of Practice

Two kinds of work, clearly separated.

Therapy

Somatic and relational therapy is psychotherapy. I provide it as a Licensed Professional Counselor to residents of Ohio, Arizona and Colorado. It can include assessment, treatment planning, and clinical care.

Coaching & Wellness

Sound healing, breathwork, coaching and workshops are not psychotherapy. They are educational and experiential practices supporting rest, body awareness and personal growth, and they are open to anyone, anywhere.

Coaching and wellness services are not a substitute for mental health treatment, diagnosis, or crisis care, and no therapeutic relationship is created by booking them. If you are unsure which one fits, start with a conversation. Sorting that out together is part of the work. In an emergency, call or text 988.
