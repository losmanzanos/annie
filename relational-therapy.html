Is this where you are?

Authenticity is easy alone.

It is in the presence of other people that the old pull to adapt comes back. Relational work is where self trust stops being a concept and becomes something you have actually done.

Self trust is built in company, not in isolation.

  • You get very agreeable exactly when you are most upset.
  • You read the room before you decide what you feel.
  • Conflict feels like a threat to the whole relationship.
  • You are navigating a structure that most therapists do not understand.
  • You want closeness and it makes you want to run.
