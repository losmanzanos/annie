Reconnect

Learning to trust your body and your nervous system again, after years of being taught to override both.

III
Is this where you are?

You have understood things
for a long time.

Insight stopped being the problem a while ago. You can explain your patterns clearly and still feel a long way from your own body. This stage is where understanding drops out of the head and into the nervous system.

The body was never the problem. It was the part that kept the record.

  • You can name what happened, but you don't feel different.
  • Rest feels unsafe, or productive-only.
  • You notice a feeling and immediately explain it away.
  • Your body sends signals you've learned to override.
  • Talking about it has started to feel like circling.

What a session is like

You don't need to know what you're doing.

01

Arriving

A short conversation. Where you are today, what your body is holding, what you'd rather not talk about. No history intake required.

02

Settling

You lie down, fully clothed, supported. Breath slows before anything else begins. Nothing is asked of you.

03

Sound

Bowls, voice, and tone move through the room and through you. Sensation shows up where you'd stopped noticing it.

04

Returning

Slow re-entry, water, and space to say anything that surfaced, or to say nothing and let it settle.
